Trigger drives the same state machine as the footswitch: empty → record → play → overdub → play. (Space / c)

With Freeze selected, the footswitch is momentary or latching depending on how you use it: tap to latch it on, hold past 400 ms and it lets go when your foot lifts. Capture always fires on the press, so the drone starts under your foot.

Holds the spectrum of what you played, not a slice of it, so nothing repeats. Shimmer sets how much the phase is randomised each frame: 0 continues the chord faithfully and can sound static, 1 is airier. Hold at 1.0 sustains forever.
